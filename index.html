Pre/Post Composer

Photos are processed on this device only. Nothing is uploaded or kept.
Add the pre-op and post-op photos Profile shots work best. You will brush out tubes and sponges, tap three landmarks on each photo, and get a matched, stacked comparison.

Backdrop paints over things standing out against the dark background (nasal tube, plastic wrap, sponge bulge) and replaces them with background. Drape paints over things lying on the head wrap (pilot balloon, sponge face) and replaces them with smooth drape. Skin under your strokes is left untouched while Protect skin is on. Pinch or scroll to zoom.
